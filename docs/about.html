About Me

My journey into software development began in 2023 with a curiosity about how websites and applications are built. What started as an interest quickly became a passion and inspired me to pursue a career in software development. Since then, I've focused on building modern, responsive, and user-friendly web applications while continuously expanding my skills. Today, I work primarily with JavaScript, React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, HTML, CSS, and Git, and I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, improve, and create software that makes a positive impact.

Before transitioning into development, I gained valuable experience in enterprise technical support and operations, where I developed strong analytical thinking, troubleshooting abilities, and a customer-focused mindset. Those experiences continue to influence the way I approach software development today. Outside of technology, I enjoy staying active, spending time outdoors, cooking, and exploring new ideas. I believe that maintaining a balance between learning, creativity, and personal interests helps me approach every challenge with curiosity, discipline, and a positive attitude.
